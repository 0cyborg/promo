Philosophy

How we think about software

🦀 Native first

Rust backend, platform WebView. No bundled Chromium, no Node runtime.

⚡ Small & fast

~5 MB binaries, ~30 MB RAM. Start instantly, stay out of the way.

🔌 ETAPI built-in

Every app ships an embedded API. AI agents and scripts can interact out of the box.

📡 Offline-first

No server, no account. Your data lives on your machine. Sync is opt-in.
